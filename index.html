About Me

My name is Racheal Pelumi Nwachukwu. I was born in Nigeria and currently live in Ogun State, Nigeria. I have roots in both Ogun State and Enugu State, giving me connections to both Yoruba and Igbo cultures.

Nigeria

Nigeria is a country in West Africa with a rich cultural heritage and diverse communities. It is home to many ethnic groups, languages, traditions, foods, and forms of music.

Ogun State is located in southwestern Nigeria, while Enugu State is in southeastern Nigeria. Having roots in both regions has given me an appreciation for the different cultures and traditions that make Nigeria unique.
