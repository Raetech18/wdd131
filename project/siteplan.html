Site name

Ajo Tracker

Ajo is the Yoruba word for a rotating savings group, also called esusu in other parts of West Africa. The name tells a visitor in two words what the site does and who it is for. "Tracker" says that the site keeps count of the money so members do not have to.

The logo is a ring of six members. One member is gold: the person whose turn it is to receive the pot. It shows the rotation that makes an ajo work.

Optional domain idea: ajotracker.ng (availability not checked).

Site purpose

Many ajo groups still track contributions in notebooks, messy spreadsheets or WhatsApp threads, which leads to disputes and missed payments. Ajo Tracker gives a group one clear place to learn how an ajo works and to manage and monitor their own.

The site will provide:

  • A summary of total contributions, outstanding payments and the group's savings position.
  • A payout schedule page that works out whose turn it is to receive the pot, based on the number of members and the rounds completed.
  • A form to add members and record contributions, saved in the browser with localStorage.
  • A short guide that explains ajo for people who are new to it.

Planned pages

  • Home: the current turn, group summary and a short explanation of ajo.
  • Tracker: member list, contribution form and outstanding payments.
  • Payout schedule: the full rotation and who receives the pot next.
  • References: sources for any text or images, linked in the footer.

Scenarios

Questions a typical visitor, such as an ajo coordinator or a member, would ask:

  • Whose turn is it to collect the pot this round?
  • Who has not paid their contribution yet, and how much is still outstanding?
  • How much has the group saved so far, and how many rounds are left?
  • I am new to ajo. How does it work, and how do I start a group?

Color schema

The palette comes from adire indigo cloth and the gold of the coins in the pot. It is used on this document.

  • Adire indigo #1B2A6B
    Headings, body text, header and footer backgrounds, and logo.
  • Pot gold #F2B632
    Accent for the current turn, buttons, the navigation bar and keyboard focus outlines. Used only as a background or outline, always with indigo text.
  • Paper #F7F8FB
    Page background and text on indigo.
  • Paid green #17724A
    Paid status messages and confirmations on the tracker pages.
  • Soft ink #4A5170
    Captions, hints and other secondary text.

Typography

Bricolage Grotesque Bold

Used for: the site name, page titles, section headings and the big numbers in the summary. Its slightly quirky shapes give the site a friendly, local feel.

Nunito Sans Regular and Bold

Used for: paragraphs, navigation, form labels, buttons and table text. It is clear at small sizes on phones.

Fallbacks: Trebuchet MS for headings, and Segoe UI or Arial for body text, both with the generic sans-serif family as a last resort.
