About Ajo Tracker

Ajo explained

How does ajo work?

Ajo, also known as esusu in parts of West Africa, is a group savings arrangement. Members agree to contribute a set amount at regular intervals. Each round, one member receives the pooled contribution until everyone has had a turn.

  1. Agree on the rules. Choose the contribution amount, frequency, members, and payout order together.
  2. Record every payment. Keep clear records so members can confirm what has been received and what remains due.
  3. Follow the rotation. Use an agreed schedule and communicate changes to all members.

Remember: Ajo Tracker is a learning project and local record-keeping demo, not a bank or payment service. It does not move or safeguard money.
